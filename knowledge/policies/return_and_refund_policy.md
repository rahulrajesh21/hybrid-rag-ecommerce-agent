# NovaGear Return and Refund Policy

## Standard Return Windows and Eligibility

### 30-Day Standard Return Window
NovaGear provides a 30-day standard return window for most physical products, beginning on the date of carrier delivery confirmation. Eligible items include unopened accessories, unused apparel, ergonomic chairs such as the ErgoChair Pro (SKU: NG-CHR-850), and everyday carry gear such as the MetroPack 25L Backpack (SKU: NG-BPK-25L) and TechSling 6L (SKU: NG-SLG-06L). To qualify for a full refund under the 30-day standard return window, products must be in their original, undamaged condition with all tags, inserts, and original packaging intact. Customers must initiate the return through the NovaGear Self-Service Portal within 30 calendar days of delivery.

### 14-Day Return Window for Opened Electronics
For consumer electronics and smart devices—including the NovaBuds Pro 2 Wireless Earbuds (SKU: NG-ANC-700), NovaSense Smart Hub (SKU: NG-HUB-01), NovaBeam Monitor Lightbar (SKU: NG-LGT-100), and NovaPower 65W GaN Charger (SKU: NG-PWR-065)—NovaGear provides a 14-day return window from the date of delivery. Opened electronic devices may be returned if they are fully functional, sanitized, and packed with all original charging cables, power adapters, and product documentation. If an opened electronic product is returned with missing accessories or damaged retail packaging, a 10% restocking fee is deducted from the total refund amount.

### Non-Returnable and Final Sale Items
Certain items sold by NovaGear are non-returnable and deemed final sale upon purchase. Non-returnable products include electronic digital gift cards, downloadable software licenses, factory-refurbished clearance inventory marked as "Final Sale," and replacement silicone ear tip packs for NovaBuds Pro 2 once their sterile packaging seal has been broken due to hygiene regulations. Defective or damaged items received from final-sale orders remain covered under the NovaGear 2-Year Limited Warranty and are eligible for repair or replacement rather than a return.

## Return Shipping and Restocking Fees

### Domestic Return Shipping Rates and Methods
For domestic orders returned within the United States, NovaGear offers prepaid carrier shipping labels through FedEx and USPS. When a customer chooses store credit or an item exchange (such as swapping an apparel size or colorway), domestic return shipping is 100% free. If a customer chooses a refund back to their original payment method, a flat return shipping fee of $7.99 is automatically deducted from the total refund amount. Customers returning freight-delivered items, such as the ErgoLift Standing Desk (SKU: NG-DSK-420), must schedule a specialized freight pickup, which carries a flat $49.00 freight return service fee.

### Condition Requirements and Warehouse Inspection Process
All returned items must arrive at the NovaGear central fulfillment facility located in Austin, Texas within 14 calendar days of Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) generation. Upon arrival, the NovaGear quality assurance team inspects the returned products within 48 to 72 hours. Items exhibiting excessive wear, physical damage, liquid ingress, or missing original serial number plates are subject to denial of refund, in which case the product will be returned to the customer at their expense.

## Refund Processing and Methods

### Refund Timelines and Original Payment Methods
Once the warehouse inspection approves a returned product, refunds are initiated immediately to the original payment method used during checkout (including Visa, MasterCard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna, or Affirm). The processing financial institution typically requires 3 to 5 business days to post the credited funds to the customer's account balance. For orders paid via installment providers such as Klarna or Affirm, future scheduled payments are cancelled and previously paid installments are refunded directly by the provider within 5 to 7 business days.

### Store Credit and Gift Card Refunds
Customers who select NovaGear Store Credit as their preferred refund method receive their digital credit balance immediately upon return inspection approval. Store credit is issued as a digital redemption voucher sent directly to the customer's registered account email address. NovaGear Store Credit never expires, carries no maintenance or transaction fees, and can be combined with promotional codes and seasonal sales across all products in the NovaGear catalog.
