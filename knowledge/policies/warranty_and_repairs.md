# NovaGear Warranty and Repair Policy

## Warranty Coverage by Product Category

### 2-Year Limited Electronics Warranty
All electronic hardware and smart devices manufactured by NovaGear—including NovaBuds Pro 2 Wireless Earbuds (SKU: NG-ANC-700), NovaSense Smart Hub (SKU: NG-HUB-01), NovaBeam Monitor Lightbar (SKU: NG-LGT-100), and NovaPower 65W GaN Charger (SKU: NG-PWR-065)—are protected by the NovaGear 2-Year Limited Electronics Warranty. This warranty covers manufacturing defects, internal component failures, unexpected battery degradation below 70% original capacity within 24 months, and wireless connectivity malfunctions under normal consumer use. The warranty term begins on the verified retail delivery date and is non-transferable to subsequent third-party owners.

### 5-Year Limited Workspace Warranty
NovaGear provides a comprehensive 5-Year Limited Workspace Warranty covering all mechanical and structural components of the ErgoLift Standing Desk (SKU: NG-DSK-420) and the ErgoChair Pro (SKU: NG-CHR-850). For the ErgoLift Standing Desk, the warranty protects the dual lifting motors, computerized control handset, electrical power supply unit, and telescoping steel frame against mechanical failure. For the ErgoChair Pro, the warranty covers the pneumatic gas lift cylinder, synchro-tilt reclining mechanism, 4D adjustable armrest brackets, and structural seat frame against manufacturing defects for a full 60 months.

### Lifetime Limited Warranty for Bags and Everyday Carry
NovaGear Everyday Carry (EDC) bags—including the MetroPack 25L Backpack (SKU: NG-BPK-25L) and the TechSling 6L (SKU: NG-SLG-06L)—are backed by the NovaGear Lifetime Limited Bag Warranty. This warranty covers structural failures resulting from factory defects for the practical lifetime of the product. Covered defects include burst structural seams, broken YKK AquaGuard zipper sliders, failed magnetic Fidlock hardware buckles, and separated shoulder harness attachment webbing. Normal cosmetic wear, fabric discoloration, and accidental puncturing are excluded from lifetime warranty coverage.

## Warranty Claim Submission Process

### Filing a Warranty Claim
To submit a warranty claim, customers must visit the NovaGear Warranty Portal at warranty.novagear.com or contact customer care at support@novagear.com. Every claim submission requires: (1) valid proof of purchase containing the original order number or authorized retailer invoice, (2) the device serial number located on the retail packaging or device casing, and (3) a brief 10-to-30-second video or photographic evidence demonstrating the malfunction or defect. NovaGear technical specialists review and respond to all warranty submissions within 1 to 2 business days.

### Replacement and Repair Procedures
When a warranty claim is approved, NovaGear will determine whether to provide factory-certified repair, a component replacement, or a brand-new replacement unit. For electronics such as the NovaBuds Pro 2 or NovaSense Hub, an advance replacement unit is shipped within 3 to 5 business days, along with a prepaid return shipping label to send the defective unit back for failure analysis. For workspace furniture, replacement motors, control boxes, or mechanical hardware are dispatched with comprehensive step-by-step installation instructions and repair tools.

## Exclusions and Unauthorized Modifications

### What Is Not Covered Under Warranty
The NovaGear Limited Warranty does not cover: (1) cosmetic blemishes, scratches, or dents resulting from daily use; (2) damage caused by accidental drops, impact, or crushing; (3) liquid ingress or moisture corrosion beyond designated IP rating limits (such as submerging NovaBuds Pro 2 beyond its IPX5 water resistance rating); (4) damage resulting from power surges, unauthorized wall adapters, or third-party charging cables; and (5) products that have been disassembled, modified, or repaired by unauthorized service centers without prior written approval from NovaGear.
