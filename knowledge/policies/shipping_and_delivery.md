# NovaGear Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Domestic Shipping Tiers and Rates

### Standard Ground Shipping
NovaGear offers Standard Ground Shipping across all 50 United States and Puerto Rico through carrier partnerships with FedEx Ground, UPS, and the United States Postal Service (USPS). Standard Ground Shipping is completely free on all domestic orders with a pre-tax merchandise subtotal of $75.00 or higher. For orders totaling under $75.00, a flat-rate shipping fee of $5.99 is applied at checkout. Orders sent via Standard Ground Shipping typically process within 24 to 48 hours and arrive at the destination address within 3 to 5 business days following fulfillment.

### 2-Day Express Shipping
For customers requiring faster delivery, NovaGear provides 2-Day Express Shipping via air freight carriers for a flat rate of $14.99 per order, regardless of parcel weight. Orders placed with 2-Day Express Shipping before 2:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST) on business days (Monday through Friday) are processed and dispatched on the same calendar day. Express packages arrive within 2 business days and include priority tracking and doorstep delivery confirmation. Note that 2-Day Express Shipping cannot deliver to P.O. Box addresses or military APO/FPO locations.

### Overnight Priority Delivery
NovaGear Overnight Priority Delivery guarantees next-business-day delivery across the contiguous United States for a flat fee of $24.99. Orders utilizing Overnight Priority Delivery must be completed prior to the daily 2:00 PM EST carrier dispatch cutoff time. Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST on Thursday will be delivered on Monday, while orders submitted on Friday after 2:00 PM EST or over the weekend will be dispatched on Monday for Tuesday morning delivery. Saturday delivery is available for select metropolitan ZIP codes upon checkout inquiry.

## Shipping Restrictions and Special Handling

### Freight Shipping for ErgoLift Standing Desks
Due to weight and physical dimensions exceeding 85 lbs, the ErgoLift Standing Desk (SKU: NG-DSK-420) is classified as an oversized freight item and cannot be delivered via standard parcel courier services. All ErgoLift Standing Desks ship via specialized threshold freight carriers. Freight shipping is free on all ErgoLift Desk orders within the contiguous United States. Freight transit typically requires 5 to 7 business days. The carrier will contact the customer via phone 24 hours prior to arrival to schedule a dedicated 4-hour delivery window, and an adult signature (age 18+) is strictly required upon curbside or threshold drop-off.

### P.O. Boxes and Military APO/FPO Addresses
NovaGear delivers to United States P.O. Boxes and military APO/FPO/DPO addresses exclusively via USPS Priority Mail. Standard parcel products, including the NovaBuds Pro 2 (SKU: NG-ANC-700), TechSling 6L (SKU: NG-SLG-06L), and NovaPower 65W GaN Charger (SKU: NG-PWR-065), are fully eligible for P.O. Box shipping. However, oversized workspace furniture, including the ErgoLift Standing Desk (SKU: NG-DSK-420) and ErgoChair Pro (SKU: NG-CHR-850), cannot be delivered to P.O. Boxes or military forwarding addresses due to USPS freight size limitations.

## Tracking, Delays, and Lost Shipments

### Tracking Notification and Status Updates
As soon as a customer order is packaged and verified at the NovaGear logistics hub in Austin, Texas, an automated tracking email is dispatched containing a direct carrier tracking link and estimated delivery window. Customers can also monitor their parcel status in real time by logging into the NovaGear Customer Portal or entering their order number and delivery postal code on the NovaGear Track Order page. Tracking events typically update within 12 hours of carrier intake scan.

### Lost, Stolen, or Damaged In-Transit Shipments
If a carrier delivery scan indicates that a NovaGear shipment has been delivered but cannot be located, or if the package arrives visibly damaged, customers must file a claim with the NovaGear Support Team within 7 calendar days of delivery. For damaged deliveries, customers should retain all outer shipping boxes and inner protective packaging, and provide clear photographic documentation. NovaGear guarantees a free reshipment or full refund for verified lost-in-transit packages following a mandatory 48-hour carrier trace investigation.
