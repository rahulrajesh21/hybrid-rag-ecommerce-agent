# NovaGear Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

## Data Collection and Device Telemetry

### NovaSense Smart Hub Telemetry and Privacy
The NovaSense Smart Hub (SKU: NG-HUB-01) is engineered using a local-first privacy architecture. All local smart home automations, Zigbee 3.0 sensor routines, Matter protocol bindings, and Thread mesh communications execute directly on the physical hub hardware within the user's home network. NovaGear does not record, sell, or analyze individual sensor activations, room temperatures, or ambient light levels. Device telemetry transmitted to NovaGear cloud servers is strictly limited to anonymized crash logs, operating system firmware versions, and connection uptime statistics to facilitate automated over-the-air (OTA) stability patches. Users may opt out of all anonymized crash telemetry via the NovaGear Mobile App privacy preferences.

### Customer Account and Transaction Data Security
When purchasing through novagear.com, NovaGear collects necessary customer billing and delivery information, including full name, shipping destination, billing address, contact email, and telephone number. Payment processing is tokenized directly through Level 1 PCI-DSS certified payment processors (Stripe and PayPal); NovaGear never stores full credit card numbers, CVV security codes, or banking credentials on its local web servers. Personal transaction details are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 encryption and maintained at rest under AES-256 standards in compliance with GDPR and CCPA regulations.

## Cookie Usage and Analytics

### Essential and Analytical Cookies
NovaGear utilizes essential browser cookies necessary for foundational storefront functions, including shopping cart persistence, customer session authentication, and secure checkout handoffs. Optional performance and analytical cookies gather aggregated, non-personally identifiable metrics on page visit velocity, navigation drop-off rates, and search query trends. Customers may modify their cookie consent preferences at any time by accessing the Cookie Preferences banner located in the storefront footer. Rejecting analytical cookies does not restrict customer access to any purchasing or account features.

## Account Security and Intellectual Property

### User Account Responsibilities
Customers registering an account on novagear.com are responsible for safeguarding their login credentials and maintaining a strong, unique password. NovaGear highly recommends enabling Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) via SMS or authenticator application (TOTP) in the account security settings. Customers must notify NovaGear security immediately at security@novagear.com if unauthorized access or fraudulent order activity is suspected. NovaGear reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate terms of service, engage in fraudulent dispute filings, or exploit promotional coupon codes.

Users are granted a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to utilize the firmware solely for the intended operation of their purchased NovaGear products.
