# NovaGear Sizing, Fit, and Dimensions Guide

## Audio and Wearable Fit

### NovaBuds Pro 2 Ear Tip Sizing and Fit Guide
The NovaBuds Pro 2 Wireless Earbuds (SKU: NG-ANC-700) include four sets of hypoallergenic silicone ear tips in sizes Extra Small (XS), Small (S), Medium (M), and Large (L). The Medium tips are pre-installed on the earbuds at the factory. Achieving an airtight acoustic seal is vital for maximizing Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) performance and deep bass response. Customers should test multiple tip sizes in each ear, as individual ear canal diameters frequently differ. To confirm an optimal fit, open the NovaGear Companion App on iOS or Android and run the built-in "Ear Tip Fit Test," which emits a localized audio chime to verify acoustic seal integrity and ambient isolation.

## Workspace Ergonomics Dimensions and Body Fit

### ErgoLift Standing Desk Dimensions and Height Clearance
The ErgoLift Standing Desk (SKU: NG-DSK-420) is engineered with a motorized adjustment range from 28 inches (71 cm) to 48 inches (122 cm). This vertical span accommodates ergonomic sit-to-stand postures for users ranging from 5'1" (155 cm) to 6'4" (193 cm) in height. 
- **Standard Desktop Size (48" x 30" / 120cm x 76cm):** Requires a minimum room clearance width of 52 inches. Recommended for dual-monitor setups up to 27 inches or single ultrawide displays up to 34 inches.
- **Spacious Desktop Size (60" x 30" / 152cm x 76cm):** Requires a minimum room clearance width of 64 inches. Ideal for dual 32-inch monitors, desktop studio audio speakers, and PC tower enclosures.
- **Under-Desk Clearance:** The minimum distance between desk legs is 42 inches for the 48" top and 52 inches for the 60" top, providing ample knee room and space for under-desk walking treadmills.

### ErgoChair Pro Adjustability and Body Proportions
The ErgoChair Pro (SKU: NG-CHR-850) is designed to accommodate individuals weighing up to 300 lbs (136 kg) with body heights between 5'2" (157 cm) and 6'5" (195 cm). 
- **Seat Dimensions:** The molded seat pan measures 20.0 inches (51 cm) in width and offers adjustable seat depth from 18.0 inches to 20.5 inches (46 cm to 52 cm) via a side lever, ensuring a 2-finger clearance between the seat edge and the back of the user's knees.
- **Seat Height Range:** The pneumatic cylinder adjusts seat height from 18.0 inches (45.7 cm) to 22.0 inches (55.8 cm) from the floor.
- **Armrest Positioning:** The 4D armrests adjust 3.5 inches vertically, 2.0 inches forward/backward, 1.2 inches inward/outward, and pivot 30 degrees to support ergonomic forearm alignment during keyboard typing.
