# NovaGear Loyalty Program and Promotions

## NovaGear Club Loyalty Tiers

### Explorer Tier (0 to 499 Lifetime Points)
The Explorer Tier is the entry level of the NovaGear Club, granted automatically to all customers upon creating a free account at novagear.com. 
- **Earning Velocity:** Members earn 1 reward point for every $1.00 spent on qualifying merchandise purchases (excluding shipping freight, taxes, and gift card purchases).
- **Core Benefits:** Access to member-only seasonal promotions, an annual $10.00 digital birthday reward voucher (valid on orders over $50 during the member's birthday month), and receipt-free warranty claim verification linked to the customer account profile.

### Innovator Tier (500 to 1,499 Lifetime Points)
Customers attain the Innovator Tier once their cumulative account balance reaches 500 reward points (equivalent to $500 in lifetime spending).
- **Earning Velocity:** Members earn an accelerated 1.25 reward points per $1.00 spent (a 25% points bonus on all catalog purchases).
- **Exclusive Perks:** Complimentary 2-Day Express Shipping on all orders over $50 (bypassing the standard $14.99 express fee), 24-hour early access to limited-edition hardware drops and colorway releases, and an upgraded $20.00 annual birthday voucher.

### Vanguard Tier (1,500+ Lifetime Points)
The Vanguard Tier represents the premier echelon of the NovaGear Club community, reserved for dedicated tech enthusiasts and workspace professionals who achieve 1,500 lifetime reward points.
- **Earning Velocity:** Members earn 1.5 reward points per $1.00 spent (a 50% points bonus on every purchase).
- **VIP Privileges:** Unrestricted free 2-Day Express Shipping on all orders with zero minimum spend requirement, dedicated VIP Concierge Support via direct email and phone priority routing, invitation to quarterly beta-testing hardware trials, and a complimentary custom-engraved annual member gift.

## Points Redemption and Monetary Value

### Point Conversion Rates and Redemption Mechanics
NovaGear Club reward points carry a straightforward, transparent cash redemption value:
- **Conversion Ratio:** **100 points = $5.00** in reward savings (each point equals $0.05 USD).
- **Redemption Increments:** Points can be redeemed at checkout in blocks of 100 points once an account accumulates a minimum threshold of 200 points ($10.00 discount value).
- **Maximum Per-Order Limit:** A maximum of 2,000 points ($100.00 discount) may be redeemed on any single transaction. Points cannot be redeemed for cash or applied to freight handling surcharges on the ErgoLift Standing Desk (SKU: NG-DSK-420). Reward points remain valid as long as the account registers at least one purchase or activity within a 12-month rolling period.

## Promotional Codes and Discounts

### Welcome Discount Code WELCOME15
New customers who register for a NovaGear account and subscribe to the NovaGear Dispatch newsletter receive a personalized welcome promotion code: **`WELCOME15`**.
- **Discount Terms:** Provides an instant **15% discount** off qualifying full-priced merchandise on the customer's first order.
- **Minimum Spend:** Requires a minimum shopping cart subtotal of $50.00 USD before shipping and taxes.
- **Exclusions:** The code `WELCOME15` strictly excludes the ErgoLift Standing Desk (SKU: NG-DSK-420), factory-refurbished clearance inventory, and pre-discounted holiday hardware bundles.

### Promo Code Stacking and Exclusions Policy
NovaGear enforces a strict single-promo policy: only one promotional discount code or automated affiliate coupon can be applied per checkout transaction. Promotional discount codes cannot be stacked together or combined with employee pricing, corporate volume discounts, or flash-sale discounts. In the event of a product return from an order where a promo code was utilized, the refund is calculated based on the actual discounted purchase price paid for the returned item.
