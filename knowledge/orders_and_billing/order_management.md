# NovaGear Order Management Guide

## Order Modifications and Cancellations

### 30-Minute Grace Window for Self-Service Cancellation
Because the NovaGear automated logistics warehouse processes and stages orders rapidly for fulfillment, orders can only be cancelled within a strict 30-minute grace window following initial checkout confirmation.
- **How to Cancel:** Within 30 minutes of placing an order, log into your NovaGear account, navigate to "Order History," select the pending order, and click the "Cancel Order" button. 
- **Automatic Refund:** An automated cancellation confirmation email will be generated immediately, and the temporary authorization hold on your payment method will release within 1 to 2 business days. Once the 30-minute window closes, orders transition directly to warehouse picking and cannot be intercepted or cancelled; customers must instead wait for parcel arrival and initiate a standard return.

### Shipping Address Corrections and Carrier Reroutes
Customers who detect an error in their submitted delivery address must contact NovaGear Support via live chat or telephone (1-800-555-6682) within 1 hour of order placement. If the order has not yet been assigned a carrier tracking number, customer support specialists can modify the recipient name, street address, apartment number, or ZIP code directly. If an order has already departed the fulfillment facility, address reroute requests must be submitted directly to the shipping carrier (FedEx Delivery Manager or UPS My Choice), which may assess an independent carrier reroute administrative fee.

## Order Tracking and Status Explanations

### Understanding Your Order Status Lifecycle
When monitoring an order through the NovaGear Customer Portal, the status badge reflects the following operational stages:
- **Unfulfilled / Payment Confirmed:** Your payment transaction was approved, fraud verification checks passed, and the order has been routed to our Austin, Texas distribution center.
- **Processing / Packing:** Warehouse technicians are picking, serializing, and packing your products.
- **In-Transit:** The package has been scanned into the carrier's sorting network; real-time milestone tracking is active.
- **Out for Delivery:** The parcel is loaded onto the local delivery vehicle for final delivery before 8:00 PM local time.
- **Delivered:** The courier has confirmed doorstep drop-off or secured an authorized signature.

### Split Shipments and Multi-Box Freight Delivery
If an order contains both standard parcel items (such as the NovaBuds Pro 2 or MetroPack 25L) and oversized workspace furniture (such as the ErgoLift Standing Desk SKU: NG-DSK-420), the order will automatically be fulfilled as a split shipment. Standard parcel items ship immediately via FedEx Ground or UPS in individual cartons, while the ErgoLift Desk ships separately via specialized freight carrier in two heavy boxes (Box 1: Tabletop; Box 2: Steel Dual-Motor Frame). Customers receive distinct tracking numbers and delivery estimates for each parcel segment.

## Damaged, Missing, or Incorrect Shipments

### Missing Items and Packing Discrepancies
Upon receiving your delivery, verify all enclosed products against the printed packing slip included inside the carton. If an item is missing from your box or if an incorrect product model or colorway was delivered in error, you must notify NovaGear Customer Support within 5 calendar days of delivery confirmation. Provide your order number, clear photographs of the packing slip, and photos of the external shipping box showing the printed carrier shipping label. NovaGear will dispatch an expedited replacement for any missing or incorrect merchandise within 24 hours at zero additional cost.
