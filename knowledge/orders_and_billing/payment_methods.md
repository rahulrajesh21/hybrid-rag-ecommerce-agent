# NovaGear Payment Methods and Billing Information

## Accepted Payment Types

### Credit and Debit Cards Accepted
NovaGear accepts all major domestic and international credit and debit cards, including Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, Diners Club, and JCB. All credit card transactions processed through novagear.com are encrypted using TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols with Level 1 PCI-DSS compliance tokenization. Customers can securely save card credentials to their encrypted NovaGear account for accelerated one-click checkout. Prepaid debit cards and gift cards issued by Visa or American Express are accepted provided they have been registered with the issuing bank with a verified billing address.

### Digital Wallets and Express Checkout
For fast, frictionless mobile and desktop transactions, NovaGear supports express digital wallet integrations, including Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, and PayPal Express Checkout. When checking out using Apple Pay or Google Pay, shipping address information and contact details are automatically populated from the user's digital wallet, bypassing manual checkout entry. PayPal transactions support both instant account balance deductions and PayPal Credit financing.

### Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) Financing via Klarna and Affirm
NovaGear partners with Klarna and Affirm to offer flexible installment financing plans on qualifying orders with a merchandise subtotal of $50.00 or greater.
- **Klarna Pay in 4:** Splits order total into four equal, interest-free payments due every 2 weeks, with the initial 25% installment charged at the time of purchase.
- **Affirm Monthly Financing:** Available for larger purchases such as the ErgoLift Standing Desk (SKU: NG-DSK-420) or ErgoChair Pro (SKU: NG-CHR-850), offering 3, 6, or 12-month financing options with interest rates starting at 0% APR depending on credit eligibility. Selecting BNPL financing does not delay fulfillment; orders ship according to standard warehouse delivery schedules.

## Invoicing, Currencies, and Sales Tax

### Supported Currencies and International Payments
The primary transaction currency for NovaGear is United States Dollars (USD). Customers shopping from international regions can view storefront catalog pricing in Canadian Dollars (CAD), Euros (EUR), and British Pounds (GBP) through automatic geolocation IP detection. Currency conversions are updated hourly based on real-time interbank foreign exchange rates. International orders are processed in the displayed localized currency with zero foreign transaction markup added by NovaGear, though third-party issuing banks may apply independent cross-border conversion fees.

### Sales Tax Calculation and Tax-Exempt Orders
Sales tax is computed automatically during checkout in accordance with United States state and municipal economic nexus taxation statutes, calculated based on the delivery destination ZIP code. State sales taxes apply to merchandise subtotals and, in certain jurisdictions, shipping freight charges. Business, educational, and non-profit institutions with valid state tax-exemption certificates can register their tax-exempt documentation by emailing tax@novagear.com prior to placing orders to enable automatic sales tax waiver at checkout.

## Payment Security and Verification

### Fraud Prevention and 3D Secure 2.0 (3DS2) Authentication
To protect consumers against fraudulent card usage and unauthorized account access, all transactions on novagear.com pass through automated fraud detection screening powered by machine-learning risk analysis. Purchases triggering unusual velocity patterns, mismatched Address Verification System (AVS) billing details, or high-risk IP addresses are subject to 3D Secure 2.0 (3DS2) biometric or SMS one-time password (OTP) verification challenges directly with the cardholder's issuing bank. Orders that fail risk verification are cancelled and authorization holds are reversed within 24 hours.
