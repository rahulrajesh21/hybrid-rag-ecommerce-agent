# NovaGear Customer Support and Escalation Directory

## Standard Customer Support Channels

### 24/7 Live Digital and Human Specialist Chat
NovaGear provides round-the-clock real-time chat support accessible directly on novagear.com and within the NovaGear Mobile Application. Customers can interact with our automated technical assistant for instant answers regarding order tracking, warranty claims, troubleshooting error codes (such as `ERR-BT-104` or `ERR-SYNC-501`), and return label generation. If a complex technical inquiry or custom order adjustment requires human intervention, customers can request a transfer to a Tier 2 Human Specialist, with average queue wait times under 3 minutes during peak operational hours.

### Email Support and Service Level Agreement (SLA)
For detailed inquiries, order amendments, or technical diagnostic reviews requiring attachments, customers can reach our dedicated support desk at **support@novagear.com**.
- **Response SLA:** The NovaGear support team guarantees a first-response SLA within **4 hours** during standard business operating hours, and within 12 hours during weekends and observed federal holidays.
- **Warranty Claims Email:** For accelerated warranty processing, direct your message and photo/video attachments to **warranty@novagear.com**.

### Dedicated Phone Support Line
NovaGear operates a toll-free customer support hotline for voice assistance:
- **Toll-Free Phone Number:** **1-800-555-NOVA** (or **1-800-555-6682**)
- **Operating Hours:**
