# NovaGear Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Product and Technical Compatibility

### Are NovaBuds Pro 2 Compatible with Both iOS and Android?
Yes. The NovaBuds Pro 2 Wireless Earbuds (SKU: NG-ANC-700) are engineered with universal Bluetooth 5.3 architecture and are fully compatible with Apple iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and ChromeOS devices. High-definition audio streaming is supported via AAC on iOS devices and high-resolution LDAC on compatible Android devices. The NovaGear Companion App is available as a free download on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, providing customizable EQ profiles, Active Noise Cancellation toggle controls, and touch sensor remapping across both mobile platforms.

### Can the NovaSense Smart Hub Function Without an Active Internet Connection?
Yes. The NovaSense Smart Hub (SKU: NG-HUB-01) features local-first processing architecture. All pre-configured local smart home automation routines, Matter-over-Thread sensor triggers, and Zigbee 3.0 mesh device bindings execute entirely within the hub's local hardware without requiring cloud connectivity. If your home broadband connection goes offline, local light switches, motion sensors, and automated schedules continue to operate seamlessly. Internet access is only required for initial device onboarding, cloud assistant integration (Amazon Alexa and Google Home remote access), and automated over-the-air firmware updates.

### What Is the Total Dynamic Weight Capacity of the ErgoLift Standing Desk?
The ErgoLift Standing Desk (SKU: NG-DSK-420) features a maximum dynamic lifting capacity of 300 lbs (136 kg). This weight rating accounts for the weight of the desktop surface itself plus all mounted desktop equipment, including heavy multi-monitor mounts, computer desktop towers, audio monitor speakers, and clamp-on accessories. The synchronized dual-motor lifting system includes built-in thermal overload protection and active gyro-based anti-collision sensors to prevent motor burnout under heavy loads.

### Is the MetroPack 25L Backpack Fully Waterproof?
The MetroPack 25L Backpack (SKU: NG-BPK-25L) is highly weather-resistant and engineered to protect sensitive electronics against severe downpours, wind-driven rain, and urban weather extremes. It is constructed from military-grade 500D Cordura ballistic nylon with a durable polyurethane (PU) water-repellent coating and features YKK AquaGuard weather-sealed zippers across all exterior compartments. While impervious to heavy rain, snow, and splashes, the backpack is not designed for complete underwater submersion or extreme water sports.

## Shipping, Delivery, and Returns

### How Do I Qualify for Free Shipping on NovaGear Orders?
Standard Ground Shipping is automatically free on all domestic United States orders with a pre-tax merchandise subtotal of $75.00 or higher. For orders totaling under $75.00, a flat-rate shipping fee of $5.99 is applied at checkout. In addition, the ErgoLift Standing Desk (SKU: NG-DSK-420) qualifies for complimentary curbside threshold freight delivery across the contiguous 48 states. Members of the NovaGear Club in the Innovator and Vanguard tiers receive free 2-Day Express Shipping as an included account benefit.

### What Is the Difference in Return Windows Between Electronics and Bags?
NovaGear enforces two distinct return windows depending on product category:
- **14-Day Return Window for Opened Electronics:** Electronic devices—including NovaBuds Pro 2 (SKU: NG-ANC-700), NovaSense Smart Hub (SKU: NG-HUB-01), NovaBeam Lightbar (SKU: NG-LGT-100), and NovaPower Charger (SKU: NG-PWR-065)—must be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery.
- **30-Day Return Window for Bags and Workspace Furniture:** Non-electronic items—including the MetroPack 25L (SKU: NG-BPK-25L), TechSling 6L (SKU: NG-SLG-06L), ErgoChair Pro (SKU: NG-CHR-850), and ErgoLift Desk (SKU: NG-DSK-420)—enjoy a full 30-day return window from the date of delivery.

### How Do I Initiate a Product Return?
To initiate a return, visit the NovaGear Self-Service Returns Portal at returns.novagear.com. Enter your NovaGear order number (e.g., `NG-104928`) and your shipping ZIP code. Select the item you wish to return and choose whether you prefer a replacement exchange, digital store credit (which includes 100% free return shipping), or a refund to your original payment method (subject to a $7.99 domestic return shipping deduction). Print the generated prepaid carrier shipping label, adhere it to your return box, and hand it to any authorized FedEx or USPS drop-off location.

## Warranty and Customer Support

### How Long Does Warranty Coverage Last on NovaGear Products?
NovaGear provides three levels of warranty protection:
- **2-Year Limited Electronics Warranty:** Covers manufacturing defects and internal component failure for NovaBuds Pro 2, NovaSense Hub, NovaBeam, and NovaPower GaN Charger.
- **5-Year Limited Workspace Warranty:** Covers steel frames, lifting motors, and mechanical hardware on the ErgoLift Standing Desk and ErgoChair Pro.
- **Lifetime Limited Bag Warranty:** Covers structural seams, zippers, and hardware buckles on the MetroPack 25L and TechSling 6L.

### What Documentation Is Required to File a Warranty Claim?
To process a warranty claim, you must submit: (1) your original NovaGear order confirmation number or a purchase receipt from an authorized retail partner, (2) the product serial number printed on the device or retail box, and (3) a brief video or photo clearly illustrating the hardware failure. Submit all details through warranty.novagear.com or email our technical support staff directly at support@novagear.com.
